# Phrase reference

This is a selective working vocabulary, not an exhaustive or authoritative transcript. The Inmu community draws on several adult works, mishearings, edits, and unrelated memes. Names below are community nicknames for roles or performers, not verified legal identities. The same performer appears in different works: do not assume every phrase by 野獣先輩 comes from *真夏の夜の淫夢*. Spellings, speaker attributions, and chapter names may vary between fan sources.

The tables describe conventional meme usage, not endorsement of the original scenes. Some original contexts are sexual or coercive; do not import those implications into a conversation. An exact match can still be accidental, particularly for ordinary phrases or numbers. Assess combinations, typography, and context before reporting a likely reference.

For タクヤさん, 二軍淫夢, クッキー☆, AI-related works, and chimera expressions, see [related phrases](related-phrases.md).
